WSCAD 5-class labeler

Load an OBJ, click a region, press its number. Export writes labels.txt (one class per vertex, OBJ order).

Z undo · drag=rotate · wheel=zoom · click=paint active class
CP = CableEntry (round cable-gland entry) OR Contact WITH real depth (screw/clamp terminal, often square -- still a CP). Flat pads/screw-heads are not CPs. Paint the whole opening/recess, whichever class it physically is.
